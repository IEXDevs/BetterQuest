Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy covers the BetterQuest Chrome extension, which adds assignment tracking, question bookmarks, and theme preferences to UT Austin's Quest student pages. BetterQuest is an independent project, not an official UT Austin or Quest service.

Quest page access and permissions

BetterQuest runs on https://quest.cns.utexas.edu/student and its subpages. Its content script reads supported page elements in your existing Quest session to provide the features below. It does not sign in for you, request your password, or collect login credentials, authentication cookies, or session tokens.

The extension requests the storage API permission to remember data locally. Its content-script page access is limited to those Quest student URLs; it does not request access to all websites, your browser history, cookies, or the downloads API.

Information accessed and stored

  • Course and assignment metadata: course names, identifiers and links; assignment names, identifiers and links; assignment groups; start dates, deadlines and timezone labels; completion status; and available assignment grade labels or percentages.
  • Cache metadata: visited Quest source-page URLs, observation and refresh timestamps, and identifiers indicating which courses and assignments have been observed. This is a limited Quest cache, not general browsing-history collection.
  • Saved questions: question identifiers and numbers, the original assignment link, associated course/assignment metadata, and saved/updated timestamps. The storage model also supports bookmark notes, if supplied.
  • Preferences: light/dark/system theme and assignment-tracking/bookmark feature switches.

Assignments are collected from supported pages you visit, not by crawling all your courses. Bookmarks are saved when you choose to save them. The current question integration does not extract PDF text, question prompts, submitted answers, solutions, response history, correctness, or question points. PDF enhancement and historical study capture/export are not enabled in the current interface.

This information is used to show your observed assignments and progress, link back to saved questions, and apply your preferences. BetterQuest does not use it for advertising, profiling unrelated to these features, or sale.

Local storage and sharing

Data is stored in chrome.storage.local in your Chrome profile, under betterquest.local.v1. BetterQuest does not use Chrome's sync storage, upload this data to a developer server, or send it to analytics, advertising, or AI services. There are no external API calls or remotely loaded services in the extension's current implementation.

The background service worker manages storage and exchanges data with BetterQuest's content scripts through local Chrome extension messages. BetterQuest does not add its own encryption to stored data; protection of your browser profile and device depends on Chrome and your operating system.

Following an assignment link opens Quest normally. Quest's own requests and handling of your account are governed by Quest and UT Austin, not this policy.

Retention, controls, and deletion

Cached assignments, bookmarks, and preferences persist across browser sessions and extension updates. There is no automatic expiration of cached records.

  • Remove a bookmark using its filled bookmark icon or the Remove action in Saved Questions.
  • Disable Assignment tracker or Question bookmarks in BetterQuest's settings to stop that feature's collection or controls. Disabling a feature does not delete previously stored data.
  • To delete all BetterQuest extension data, remove BetterQuest from chrome://extensions. Chrome clears the extension's local storage when it is uninstalled. Disabling the extension, clearing browsing history, or clearing Quest's website data is not the same as deleting extension storage.

Deleting a bookmark does not delete its assignment from the assignment cache. BetterQuest does not change or delete records on Quest. Any separately saved files or browser-profile backups must be deleted separately; extension removal does not erase those copies. Because the developer does not receive your local cache, the developer cannot remotely retrieve or delete it.

Policy hosting and contacting us

This policy page is a static page intended for GitHub Pages hosting. It contains no analytics, forms, cookies, external fonts, or scripts. When hosted there, GitHub may process normal website request information, including your IP address, under its privacy statement. Visiting the policy does not give the host access to BetterQuest's local extension data.

For privacy questions, contact the BetterQuest maintainer at markord946@gmail.com. If you email us, your email provider and the recipient's email provider handle your address and the message you choose to send. Do not include passwords, session tokens, or private homework responses. Email correspondence is separate from the extension's local data; contact us to request deletion of correspondence we hold, subject to any necessary legal retention.

We will update this page and its last-updated date when BetterQuest's data practices change.

BetterQuest Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: October 9, 2026

This policy covers the BetterQuest Chrome extension and this public website.

On this page

BetterQuest is an independent IEXDevs student project that adds optional organization and appearance tools to UT Austin Quest. Coursework the extension remembers stays in your Chrome profile. Limited de-identified diagnostics may leave the device for issue reporting, and future EasyAccess support is described separately below.

01

Information stored by the extension

BetterQuest uses the single Chrome storage permission. Its local state is stored under betterquest.local.v1 and may include:

  • theme and feature preferences;
  • course and assignment metadata visible on Quest, including titles, source identifiers, URLs, deadlines, and completion labels;
  • a record of which course pages were observed and when they were last observed;
  • question identifiers, question numbers, source links, bookmark status, and bookmark notes; and
  • locally cached study records created by enabled features.

This information remains in Chrome storage on your device. BetterQuest does not currently operate an account system or upload this local coursework state as an account library. The diagnostics described below are a separate, limited data flow.

02

What BetterQuest can see on Quest

The content script runs only on quest.cns.utexas.edu/student pages. It reads the course, assignment, deadline, status, and question identifiers already visible in the page so it can build the enabled overview and bookmark features.

BetterQuest does

Enhance pages you visit and cache the limited metadata described above.

BetterQuest does not

Read or store your passwords, UT login credentials, submitted answers, response text, or solution text. It does not submit work, change grades, or automate assessments.

BetterQuest does not scan every course automatically. An assignment appears in the overview only after the relevant Quest page has been visited and observed.

03

Website and network requests

The extension contains its own feature code and does not use an advertising tracker. Quest itself continues to make the requests needed to operate the page you are using. When diagnostic and aggregate measurement features are enabled, they use IEXDevs services only for the limited purposes below.

Visiting this website sends ordinary requests to our hosting provider. Security and operational logs may include your IP address, browser type, requested path, and request time. We use those logs to deliver, protect, and troubleshoot the site.

04

Error and issue diagnostics

When diagnostics are enabled, BetterQuest may send a de-identified user-submitted issue report or an automatic extension error report after the extension detects an unexpected failure. A report may include the error category, affected feature, extension and browser version, page type, time, and sanitized technical context needed to reproduce the problem.

Diagnostic reports do not include passwords, submitted answers, coursework text, solution text, names, account identifiers, or the contents of bookmark notes. If you type information into a report yourself, the submitted text is handled as support correspondence.

05

EasyAccess and future account sync

EasyAccess is the unified IEXDevs login system and is currently available only in UTMAX. BetterQuest does not currently offer EasyAccess login. If it is added, the interface and this policy will identify which compatible preferences or product data can be stored on IEXDevs servers and shared across IEXDevs services after you create or use an account.

06

De-identified product analytics

When enabled, IEXDevs may collect de-identified measurements such as coarse general location, approximate connection quality or internet speed, API request counts and hourly usage, product version, and sanitized error categories.

Cross-product aggregates may describe high-level schedule or degree-completion trends, frequently used or requested courses, and popular majors, minors, or certificates. BetterQuest does not contribute raw answers, coursework text, names, account IDs, or complete student records to these aggregates.

De-identified aggregate results may appear in public reports and restricted internal developer dashboards. These reports are designed so an individual user cannot reasonably be identified from them.

07

Sharing and third parties

We do not sell, rent, or use your information for advertising. Our website host and diagnostic delivery infrastructure may process ordinary requests and the limited de-identified reports described above.

If you choose the contact link, you leave BetterQuest for the IEXDevs contact form. That form collects the name, email, and message you submit, uses Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent abuse, and uses an email provider to deliver the message. We retain contact correspondence only as reasonably necessary to answer it, maintain service records, or address abuse.

08

Your choices and deletion

You can disable assignment or bookmark features from BetterQuest settings. You can delete local data by removing the extension and clearing its extension data in Chrome. Clearing ordinary Quest website data alone may not remove Chrome extension storage.

BetterQuest is intended for college students and is not directed to children under 13. We may update this policy when the product or its data practices change; the effective date at the top will change with it.
